        Shadow Exit Scenarios

        These versioned scenarios are simulations only. They never place trades or change actual positions, decisions, balances, risk checks, or portfolio metrics. Tracking starts with the first fresh observed market mark; historical candles and intrabar highs or lows are not used.

        How to read these scenarios

        • Fixed take-profit: triggers at the first fresh mark meeting its target gain from the fee-inclusive entry basis.
        • Trailing stop: arms after +5%; triggers when a later fresh mark falls the selected amount from the observed high-water mark.
        • Tracking: the rule has not triggered. Triggered hypothetically: the rule would have exited; no trade was placed.
        • Estimated net P&L: modeled after 0.10% slippage and a 0.25% exit fee; spread is unavailable, so this is not a guaranteed fill.
